# Hero image prompts

Prompts used to generate the landing hero paintings (ChatGPT image generation, September 2026). The wide temple painting became `apps/web/public/hero-temple.jpg`; the portrait cut became `hero-temple-m.jpg`.

## 1. Reference prompt (the loggia painting)

Given to ChatGPT first. It produced the Italian loggia scene that set the style; every Seshat prompt below keeps its structure.

```
A wide cinematic oil painting in the style of 19th-century German Romanticism (Franz Ludwig Catel, Caspar David Friedrich). View from inside a dark, weathered classical stone loggia with tall rounded arches and heavy columns framing the scene on the left and right. Through the arches: an Italian twilight landscape — rolling dark green hills, cypress trees, a distant lake or sea, a warm orange-pink glow on the horizon fading into deep indigo blue sky, one faint star. In the lower left, a small group of five or six men in Renaissance-era dress sit closely around a small wooden table, faces lit by a single oil lamp, deep in quiet conversation. In the lower right, a lone seated figure in dark clothes looks at a softly glowing laptop screen, the only modern element. Strong chiaroscuro, muted earthy palette, visible canvas texture and fine craquelure, soft atmospheric haze. The center of the image is open sky and landscape, left empty for text. Moody, contemplative, timeless. Ultra wide 16:9, high detail.
```

Through the columns: a Nile twilight landscape — a still river reflecting the sky, a line of date palms, distant low desert hills, a warm amber-rose glow on the horizon fading into deep indigo blue sky, one faint seven-pointed star. ## 3. Follow-ups on the night painting (lighter versions)

Pasted as follow-ups in the same ChatGPT thread so the composition stayed put.

### 3a. Late afternoon

```
Keep this exact composition, characters, columns and painting style. Only change the time of day: it is now late afternoon, about an hour before sunset. The sun is still above the horizon, low and golden, casting long warm light across the river and stone floor. The sky is pale gold near the horizon fading into soft blue, no deep indigo, no stars. The temple interior is brighter, warm sunlight raking across the hieroglyph columns, gentle shadows instead of heavy darkness. The oil lamp by the scribes is still there but barely noticeable in daylight. The laptop screen glows softly. Overall lighter, airier, more luminous, but still the same muted earthy oil-painting palette with canvas texture and craquelure. Center sky and river still left open for text. Ultra wide 16:9.
```

Variant: replace "an hour before sunset" with "golden hour, sun just touching the horizon" for a tone in between.

### 3b. Bright daytime (for the light theme)

```
Keep this exact composition, characters, columns and painting style, but make it a bright, airy daytime scene. Mid-morning light, sun high enough that the whole temple interior is softly lit, no dark shadowed corners. The stone columns are warm sand and cream colored, hieroglyphs clearly visible in gentle light. The sky is pale, luminous, almost washed-out: soft cream near the horizon fading into a light hazy blue, no orange sunset, no indigo, no stars. The Nile is a calm pale silver-blue mirror. Distant hills are light, dusty beige. The scribes and the laptop figure are fully visible in daylight, no oil lamp glow needed. Overall palette is light and warm like aged paper: cream, sand, pale ochre, soft blue, with the same visible canvas texture and fine craquelure. High-key, bright, contemplative. Center sky and river left open for text. Ultra wide 16:9.
```

If still too dark, append: "high-key, overexposed, faded like an old sun-bleached painting".

### 3c. Early morning, just after sunrise (the middle ground)

```
Keep this exact composition, characters, columns and painting style, but change it to early morning, just after sunrise. The sun is low but already above the horizon, filling the scene with soft, diffused golden light. Light morning mist hangs over the Nile. The temple interior is gently lit, no deep black shadows, columns in warm sand and honey tones with hieroglyphs clearly readable. The sky is pale peach near the horizon fading into a soft powder blue, no dark indigo, no stars. The river is a calm pale gold mirror. The scribes' oil lamp is still there but faint. Overall palette light and warm like aged paper: cream, sand, pale ochre, soft blue. Same visible canvas texture and fine craquelure. Luminous, calm, contemplative, not dark. Center sky and river left open for text. Ultra wide 16:9.
```

If too bright, replace "just after sunrise" with "sunrise, sun just clearing the hills".

## 4. Alternative compositions

A few scribes in white linen sit on reed mats reading unrolled scrolls, one climbs a wooden ladder to reach a high shelf. A single figure in dark modern clothes stands in the aisle reading from a glowing tablet, the only modern element. Muted warm palette of cream, sand, honey and faded blue, visible canvas texture and fine craquelure. Center of the image is a calm open wall of soft light, left empty for text. Contemplative, scholarly, timeless. Ultra wide 16:9, high detail.
